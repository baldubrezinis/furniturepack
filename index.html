Furniture Pack for Autodesk Inventor

Version 2.1 · Inventor 2027 · by Andrej

Furniture Pack builds frameless carcass furniture in Inventor from a few values: kitchen base and wall units, wardrobes, chests of drawers, sideboards and bookcases. It cuts the holes for the hardware, assigns materials and edge banding, and writes a cut list for Excel.

Getting started

  1. Make a new part (File › New › Part), or a new assembly and save it.
  2. Open the Furniture tab and click Cabinet. The cabinet window opens and the cabinet parameters are added to the document.
  3. Set the sizes and options on the tabs and click Build / Update.
  4. Change any value and click Build / Update again: the cabinet is rebuilt.
  5. Save the document and click Cut list: an Excel workbook (.xlsx) and a CSV file are written next to the document.

In a part every panel and every kind of hardware is a solid body. In an assembly every panel is its own part file (<Assembly>_<Panel>.ipt, next to the assembly) and the hardware parts are placed as components, so the bill of materials counts them. The assembly must be saved before the first build.

The cabinet window

TabValues
SizeWidth, height (without legs), depth; base: legs, plinth or none, leg / plinth height, plinth set-back.
ConstructionBoard thickness; top between or over the sides; bottom under or between the sides; back: overlay (nailed on), in a groove, inset (thick back) or none, with its thickness and groove.
FrontsOverlay or inset fronts; 0–2 doors; 0–6 drawers (fronts only) and their height; gaps; concealed 35 mm hinges: automatic count or 2–5 per door, hinge side, distance from the door ends. Hinges are moved away from shelves automatically.
Inside1–5 vertical sections with partitions and the number of shelves in each section.
HardwareJoints: cam locks (15 mm), confirmat screws 7×50 or none; shelf support holes at the shelves or a full 32 mm row; wall hangers; show or hide the hardware in the model.
MaterialsFor the carcass, shelves, back, doors, drawer fronts and plinth: board, edge band and banded edges (none, front, front + back, front + ends, all). Premilling adds a set amount per banded edge to the cut sizes.

Press Enter to build, Esc to close. The window language (English / Lietuvių) can be changed at the bottom of the window; the ribbon follows after Inventor is restarted.

Boards and edge bands

The lists are in %APPDATA%\FurniturePack\Materials (Materials › Edit lists… opens the folder):

  • Boards.csv: name; thickness (mm); colour (#RRGGBB); Inventor appearance (optional); Inventor material (optional)
  • EdgeBands.csv: name; thickness (mm); colour (#RRGGBB); Inventor appearance (optional)

Open them in Excel or Notepad and add your own lines. A board chosen from the list also sets its thickness (carcass → board thickness, back → back thickness, doors → front thickness), its colour and its Inventor material. Any other name can be typed into the window as well.

Coordinates

X = width, Y = height (floor = 0), Z = depth. The front of the carcass is at Z = 0 and the carcass goes towards −Z; overlay fronts are in +Z.

Licence

Furniture Pack checks the licence with the Autodesk App Store the first time a command is used in an Inventor session. Sign in to your Autodesk account in Inventor (top right corner) with the account that bought the app. After a successful check the app also works offline for 14 days.

Troubleshooting

  • No Furniture tab: Tools › Add-Ins, select Furniture Pack and tick Loaded/Unloaded and Load on Startup.
  • "Save the assembly first": in an assembly the panels are saved as files next to it, so the assembly needs a folder.
  • The cut list cannot be written: close the previous cut list in Excel.
  • The log file is %APPDATA%\FurniturePack\FurniturePack.log. Please attach it when you contact support.

Uninstall

Use Settings › Apps in Windows (or the Autodesk App Store installer). Your lists and settings in %APPDATA%\FurniturePack are kept; delete that folder to remove them too.
