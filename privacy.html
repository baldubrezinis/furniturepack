Furniture Pack for Autodesk Inventor – Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026. Publisher: Andrej.

What data the app uses

  • Autodesk user ID (licence check). When a Furniture Pack command is first used in an Inventor session, the app reads the ID of the Autodesk account that is signed in to Inventor and sends it, together with the app's ID, to the Autodesk App Store (apps.autodesk.com) to check that this account bought the app. Autodesk answers only whether the licence is valid. Autodesk's own privacy statement applies to that service.
  • Files on your computer. The app keeps its settings (language), the board and edge band lists, a log file and the date of the last successful licence check in %APPDATA%\FurniturePack. The log contains the names of your documents and the sizes of the cabinets you build. These files never leave your computer.
  • Your models and cut lists stay in the folders you save them to.

The app collects no other personal data, has no analytics or tracking, and does not send your designs anywhere. The publisher does not receive any of the data above.

Third parties

The only service the app contacts is the Autodesk App Store entitlement service, operated by Autodesk, which protects the data under its own privacy statement. No data is sold or shared with anyone else.

Keeping and deleting data

The local files stay until you delete them. To delete everything, uninstall the app and delete the folder %APPDATA%\FurniturePack. The licence check stores nothing with the publisher. To stop the licence check, uninstall the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the publisher through the app's page on the Autodesk App Store.

Privatumo politika (lietuviškai)

Licencijos patikra. Pirmą kartą per Inventor seansą paleidus Furniture Pack komandą, priedas Autodesk App Store (apps.autodesk.com) siunčia prie Inventor prisijungusios Autodesk paskyros ID ir priedo ID. Taip patikrinama, ar ši paskyra įsigijo priedą. Autodesk atsako tik tai, ar licencija galioja.

Failai jūsų kompiuteryje. Nustatymai, plokščių ir briaunų juostų sąrašai, įrašų failas ir paskutinės sėkmingos licencijos patikros data saugomi aplanke %APPDATA%\FurniturePack ir iš kompiuterio niekur nesiunčiami.

Kitų asmens duomenų priedas nerenka, analitikos ir sekimo nėra, jūsų modeliai niekur nesiunčiami. Autorius jokių šių duomenų negauna.

Ištrynimas. Pašalinkite priedą ir ištrinkite aplanką %APPDATA%\FurniturePack.

Kontaktai. Su autoriumi susisiekite per priedo puslapį Autodesk App Store.
